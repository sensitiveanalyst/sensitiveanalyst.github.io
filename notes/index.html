Notes

Rough algorithm sketches. Notes 01-06 are not implemented. Notes 07-10 say in their status line which parts are implemented and tested. Each note states the goal, the model, the algorithm in pseudocode, the stability or consistency argument, and what is unresolved. Treat the claims as a reading list and a design starting point, not as verified results. Claims marked (literature) are recalled from memory and need checking before they are cited.
