Backend: control systems suite

Original implementations, written from the published methods. No code was copied from another repository.

backend/
  control/
    kalman.py      KF, EKF, UKF
    lyapunov.py    Lyapunov equation, LQR, V-dot checks, pendulum swing-up
    so3.py         hat, vee, exp, log
    geometric.py   SO(3) attitude controller, SE(3) quadrotor controller
    smooth.py      smooth replacements for kinks, smooth torque-bound cost, STL robustness
    continuity.py  find jumps and kinks in a sampled signal
    pmp.py         Pontryagin minimum principle by shooting (free final time, continuation)
    geometric_pmp.py  geometric PMP for rigid-body attitude (Lie-Poisson reduced)
    notes/         theoretical pipelines (01-06 sketches, 07-10 outlines with status lines)
  tests/

Run the tests:

cd backend
pip install -r requirements.txt
python -m pytest

References: Lee, Leok, McClamroch, "Geometric tracking control of a quadrotor UAV on SE(3)", CDC 2010; Julier and Uhlmann on the unscented transform; Barrau and Bonnabel on the invariant EKF; Mahony, Hamel, Pflimlin on nonlinear complementary filters on SO(3).

Known limits: - The SE(3) controller omits the desired angular velocity and acceleration terms, so use it for slowly varying references. - The SE(3) controller's desired-attitude normalisations are smoothed by default (thrust_eps=0.02, heading="tilt_twist"); pass thrust_eps=0, heading="projection" for the paper's exact construction. The singularities move to downward-thrust commands; stability of the smoothed variant is checked in simulation, not proven. - PMP shooting finds normal extremals only, needs a costate guess, and has no second-order (conjugate point) test. Convergence for large rotations or small smoothing widths may need angle_steps > 1 or continuation. - The extremals are first-order optimal (checked by a KKT test); global optimality is not proven for non-convex cases.
