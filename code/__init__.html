geometric  Attitude control on SO(3) and a quadrotor position controller on SE(3).

smooth     Smooth replacements for kinks: |x|, max/min, clip, switches, bang-bang.
continuity Detect jumps and kinks in a sampled signal.
pmp        Pontryagin minimum principle by indirect shooting (Euclidean).
geometric_pmp  Geometric PMP for rigid-body attitude on SO(3) (Lie-Poisson reduced).

"""
